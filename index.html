No. Every question is original and aligned with the exam guide. They are not live or retired AWS exam items.

After submitting a test, you can review every correct answer, your selection, and a concise explanation.

Does using a hint affect my score?

No. Hints do not change your score. They are designed to help you recall the relevant concept.

Is my progress saved?

Yes. Your answers and current question are stored automatically in this browser.

What score should I target?

The real exam uses a scaled passing score of 700. For practice, aim for at least 80 percent without hints.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. The question palette, controls, and answer review adapt to smaller screens.
